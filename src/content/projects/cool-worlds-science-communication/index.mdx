As a video editor and content producer for the Cool Worlds YouTube channel, I collaborate with professional astrophysicists to translate active research topics into accessible, visually compelling narratives. Over the past several years, I have edited and produced 30+ long-form videos and projects reaching over 10 million cumulative views worldwide.

My role also includes storyboarding, visual sequencing, and narrative pacing for topics such as gravitational lensing, exoplanet detection, and cosmological modeling. That work requires deciding which technical details and caveats must remain clear to non-technical audiences.

This work has fundamentally shaped how I design technical systems and research pipelines: documentation, clarity of intent, and the ability to explain why something works are treated as core engineering requirements rather than afterthoughts.

## Worldwide Reach With Technical Rigor
**Insight:** Each episode is structured to keep astrophysical rigor while reducing cognitive load for non-specialists.

For each episode, I identify which equations, assumptions, and caveats must survive the edit. My highest-performing edit, reported at 2.7M views, examines quantum immortality: a speculative idea associated with the many-worlds interpretation, in which possible quantum outcomes occur across branches. It considers the claim that a version of an observer might always survive in some branch and therefore never subjectively experience death. The episode presents this as philosophical speculation, not established or testable science.

*The Strangest Idea in Science: Quantum Immortality*

## Science Communication
**Insight:** Timing edits, visual emphasis, and transition density are tuned to match topic complexity in real time.

This was my first editing project for the channel. It introduces the Fermi Paradox: why we have not observed extraterrestrial civilizations given the universe's scale and the number of potentially habitable planets. It covers proposed explanations including the Great Filter, the Rare Earth hypothesis, and the possibility that advanced civilizations deliberately avoid contact.

https://youtu.be/7TAOMvW5LaQ?si=5GikLY-6CU-ujVSa
*Is Messaging Aliens a Bad Idea?*

## Production System For Repeatable Quality
**Insight:** A repeatable editorial pipeline enables consistent quality across long-form episodes with different scientific domains.

This was the first of my edits to pass 1M views. The episode explores Earth's future over the next billion years, including solar evolution, geological processes, and possible human intervention. It discusses increasing solar luminosity, a potential runaway greenhouse effect, eventual ocean loss, and the long-term prospects for life. These are long-range scenarios, so the edit balances accessible storytelling with the uncertainty of projections.
